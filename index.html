About GeoPothole Jaipur

Citizen Road Safety & Pothole Reporting Initiative

Why this app was created: Road potholes and fractures cause severe vehicular damage, traffic delays, and fatal two-wheeler accidents across our city streets every day.

Many hazardous potholes go unaddressed simply because traditional municipal reporting takes too much time or gets lost in paperwork. GeoPothole provides a 10-second reporting portal for every citizen.

How It Works:

  • Geo-Tagged Documentation: Photos are automatically linked to exact GPS street coordinates.
  • Instant Engineering Dispatch: Submissions are logged directly into municipal road repair databases.
  • Live Public Transparency: Citizens can view existing reported potholes on the interactive city map to avoid duplicate reports and check live repair status.
