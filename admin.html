Repair Dispatch Protocol

PWD SOP
  • Site Photo Check: Verify the uploaded photo and cross-reference nearby street landmarks before assigning crews.
  • Priority Road Repair: High-severity defects (7+) along bus/arterial roads require same-day bitumen patching.
  • Duplicate Grouping: Reports within 20 meters are merged automatically into a single work order with citizen votes.
  • Lifecycle Status: Set status to In Review when dispatching crews, and Repaired after asphalt compaction.
